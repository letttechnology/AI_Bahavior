### Gloss Priority Chain (documented in CLAUDE.md and implemented in code)

The code consistently follows this priority (first non-null wins):
1. **`userTokenGloss`** -- token-level override (this verse/position only)
2. **`userLexemeGloss`** -- lexeme-level override (user's preferred gloss everywhere the word occurs)
3. **`token.gloss`** -- server-resolved auto gloss
4. **`transGloss`** / `lexiconGloss` -- translation alignment or lexicon source gloss (as fallback)
